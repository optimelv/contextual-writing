# Write academic content

1. Read the shared [context gate](../contextual-writing/references/context-gate.md), [evidence rules](../contextual-writing/references/evidence-and-preservation.md), and [language rules](../contextual-writing/references/language-and-register.md).
2. Read [academic writing](references/academic-writing.md).
3. Establish the deliverable, discipline or course, language, audience or assessment level, source material, citation system, required structure, and academic-integrity constraints from the supplied context.
4. When sources, notes, data, or a supported claim set are already supplied, draft or revise directly. Do not require a research intake merely because the text is academic. Use [the academic-writing intake](assets/academic-writing-intake.md) only for missing fields that would change the argument, evidentiary basis, or required format.
5. Select the appropriate mode: `paragraph`, `abstract`, `introduction`, `literature synthesis`, `method`, `results`, `discussion`, `conclusion`, or `revision`.
6. Preserve citations, quotations, attribution, hedging, methods, limitations, numerical values, discipline-specific terminology, and categorical meaning across languages. Keep the distinction between a source's position, the author's inference, and a demonstrated result explicit. When no exact established translation exists, retain the source term or add a short clarification rather than replacing it with a broader or different category.
7. Never invent citations, quotations, page references, data, observations, empirical work, methodological choices, results, or author experience. Mark unsupported material as a gap, question, or proposed wording rather than writing it as fact.
8. Check that the reasoning fits the discipline and section: claims have support, limits remain visible, causal strength is not inflated, and the conclusion does not exceed the evidence. In a literature synthesis, verify that the body is organized by the analytical issue rather than by one sequential source capsule per author; a comparative final sentence alone does not repair an author-ordered body. Then run a sentence-level language pass for subject-verb agreement, case, modifier attachment, spelling, punctuation, and citation placement without weakening the evidence locks. In author-led reporting clauses, explicitly match the reporting verb to the grammatical subject: a single cited author takes a singular verb; multiple named authors or an appropriate plural construction take a plural verb.
9. For assessed or submitted text, apply the shared [submission-readiness gate](../contextual-writing/references/submission-readiness.md). Recount hard limits after the final edit and keep drafting caveats outside the scholarly artifact unless the required genre calls for an explicit limitations statement.

This skill owns the final scholarly text. Use [research content](../write-research-content/SKILL.md) as an evidence layer when current facts, source discovery, verification, or a bounded research brief is needed. For a literature review requiring systematic search, a paper pipeline, methodology design, formal citation-integrity work, or deep source synthesis, check whether `$academic-research-suite` is available and hand that research layer to it if it is. If unavailable, state the limitation and continue only with the supplied or safely verifiable material. Use [edit and humanize](../edit-and-humanize/SKILL.md) only as a preservation-focused review layer.
