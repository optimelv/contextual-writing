# Write commercial content

1. Read the shared [context gate](../contextual-writing/references/context-gate.md), [evidence rules](../contextual-writing/references/evidence-and-preservation.md), and [language rules](../contextual-writing/references/language-and-register.md).
2. Read [commercial content](references/commercial-content.md). For customer, buyer, or partner sales email and outreach, also read [sales outreach](references/sales-outreach.md).
3. Resolve offer, audience, buyer stage, problem, desired action, channel, proof, constraints, and brand voice from available context.
4. Ask the missing fields from [the commercial intake](assets/commercial-intake.md) only when they change the angle or claim strength.
5. Distinguish sourced company facts, customer evidence, field signal, benchmark, inference, and proposed positioning.
6. Preserve useful persuasion. Remove unsupported hype, not the commercial purpose.
7. Keep customer-facing copy free of internal strategy, private notes, opportunity probability, margins, negotiation posture, and unverified competitive claims.
8. Return draft-only content unless the user separately authorizes sending, posting, sharing, CRM updates, or external draft creation.
9. Before returning recipient-visible or submitted copy, apply the shared [submission-readiness gate](../contextual-writing/references/submission-readiness.md). Enforce hard limits, keep internal uncertainty notes outside the artifact, and verify one semantic action when the brief requests one.

When `$sales:index` is available, use it first for every real seller, customer, prospect, partner, account, opportunity, or other commercial-intent workflow, even when evidence is already supplied. For known-recipient research-to-outreach, its company-research workflow is the preferred specialist path. Use this skill as the fallback when Sales is unavailable and as the reader-fit editing layer after the specialist workflow. It can directly own general marketing, website, product, and brand copy when no Sales-index workflow applies. Use [research content](../write-research-content/SKILL.md) for current company or competitor evidence.
