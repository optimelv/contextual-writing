# Editing modes

## Audit

Return prioritized findings with location, reason, risk, and recommended action. Do not alter the text. Separate required corrections from optional style choices.

## Minimal edit

Default mode. Change the smallest unit that improves accuracy, clarity, density, register, reader fit, or naturalness. Preserve paragraph structure unless it causes a real problem.

## Full rewrite

Use only when requested or when structure cannot support the purpose. Preserve every protected claim and persona element. Show material assumptions and unresolved gaps.

## Compression and expansion

Compression removes repetition and lower-value detail before removing necessary evidence or qualifiers. Expansion adds explanation only from supplied or verified material. Never fill space with generic framing or invented specificity.

## Localization and translation review

Preserve meaning, authority, register, terminology, and relationship. Adapt idiom, form of address, date and number conventions, and institutional terms. Do not translate word for word when the target language requires another structure. Do not silently change jurisdiction or legal effect.

Treat operative legal, contractual, regulatory, policy, technical specification, and compliance text as protected by default. If the user asks to humanize it, preserve the original and offer a separate plain-language explanation unless an authorized legal owner supplies approved replacement wording.

## Voice matching

Use only permissioned user-authored samples. Extract a transparent profile and apply it as a soft constraint. Do not reproduce distinctive living-author style on request when a high-level characterization would suffice.

## Null edit

If the text already fits its purpose, return it unchanged and state why. Do not manufacture changes to demonstrate activity.

## Detector requests

Decline claims that the workflow can bypass or defeat AI detection. Offer legitimate editing for clarity, accuracy, audience fit, and authentic user voice instead.
