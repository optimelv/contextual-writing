# Humanizing and quality assurance

## Objective

Improve truthfulness, reader fit, clarity, density, register, and voice. Do not optimize for detector scores. Do not rewrite a fit-for-purpose text merely to make it different.

## Diagnose contextually

Look for patterns in clusters and in relation to the genre:

- inflated significance, unsupported praise, or promotional claims
- vague attribution or unsourced specificity
- filler openings, generic conclusions, and chatbot residue
- superficial explanatory tails that add no evidence
- formulaic contrasts, forced groups, false ranges, or synonym cycling
- repeated signposting, headings, or inline labels that duplicate content
- uniform rhythm, manufactured punchlines, faux aphorisms, or fake-candid questions
- unexplained jargon, bureaucratic nominal style, or legalistic wording outside legal context
- translated syntax, inconsistent dialect, or inconsistent form of address
- invented personality, anecdotes, motives, emotion, or first-person experience
- register mismatch, such as de-persuasifying sales copy or casualizing legal text

A single phrase, formal word, passive sentence, em dash, heading, or polished paragraph proves nothing. Quotations, code, technical specifications, legal wording, names, and templates need false-positive protection.

## Editing modes

Audit: return prioritized findings and leave the text unchanged.

Minimal edit, default: change only passages with a concrete problem. Preserve clean sections.

Full rewrite: use only when the user requests it or structure is fundamentally unfit. Preserve the evidence and persona ledgers.

Null edit: if no change improves the stated outcome, say the text is already fit for purpose.

## Review sequence

1. Lock claims, sources, persona, and required structure.
2. Identify audience, language, register, and change budget.
3. Diagnose problems with local evidence.
4. Revise the smallest useful unit.
5. Compare with the original ledger.
6. Read for naturalness in the actual channel.
7. Stop when the next change has no clear benefit.

For material changes, explain the reason or show a before and after pair when the user requests review transparency.
