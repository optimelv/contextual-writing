# Write research content

1. Read the shared [context gate](../contextual-writing/references/context-gate.md), [evidence rules](../contextual-writing/references/evidence-and-preservation.md), and [submission readiness](../contextual-writing/references/submission-readiness.md).
2. Read [lightweight research](references/lightweight-research.md).
3. Select one mode: `quick brief`, `academic-light`, `legal-light`, `company`, `product or market`, or `fact-check`.
4. Resolve the question or decision, scope, geography or jurisdiction, effective date, audience, source standard, depth, format, and deadline. Use [the research brief](assets/research-brief-template.md) only for missing material fields.
5. Search authoritative and primary sources first. Add independent or community evidence only when it answers a different question or closes a real gap.
6. Keep a claim ledger. Verify each citation exists and supports the exact claim. Distinguish source statements, facts, inferences, assumptions, and unknowns.
7. Search for counter-evidence and disclose conflicts, limitations, and transfer constraints.
8. Produce the requested brief or hand grounded materials back to the owning writing skill. For a recipient-visible or hard-limited research artifact, run the submission-readiness gate after the final edit, keep claim-ledger and internal uncertainty notes outside the submitted copy, and recount with the destination's own counter when available. For scholarly prose, return to [academic content](../write-academic-content/SKILL.md), which owns the final academic deliverable.

When the user needs a literature review requiring systematic search, systematic review, academic paper pipeline, methodology design, citation integrity workflow, or deep source synthesis, check whether `$academic-research-suite` is available. If available, hand off the research layer to it and return materials to [academic content](../write-academic-content/SKILL.md) for the final scholarly text. If unavailable, either provide only the safe lightweight scope supported here or explain the missing capability and smallest viable fallback. Never claim the specialist is installed without verifying it. For high-stakes legal conclusions, provide research support only, identify jurisdiction and date, and recommend qualified review. Do not present this plugin as legal advice.
