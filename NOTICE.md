# Notices and conceptual sources

Contextual Writing was independently authored. Its context-aware editing design was informed by public writing resources and the following open-source projects:

- `blader/humanizer`, MIT License: https://github.com/blader/humanizer
- `marmbiz/humanizer-de`, mixed notices described by the project: https://github.com/marmbiz/humanizer-de

The plugin does not copy their pattern catalogues, examples, or file structure. Transferable ideas were reformulated as original context gates, preservation rules, null-edit behavior, and evaluation requirements.

- Purdue Online Writing Lab: https://owl.purdue.edu/
