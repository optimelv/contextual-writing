# Behavioral regression scenarios

Use these scenarios for forward-testing. The contracts evaluate behavior, not exact wording.

## Context gates

### Incomplete consulting CV

Request: `I need a CV for consulting.`

Contract:

- ask one compact form for target role or firm, market, language, career stage, evidence, and format
- do not promise one page or a country-specific layout before those fields are known
- do not draft achievements

### Sparse Bachelor CV

Request supplies a Bachelor student, two internships, two extracurricular roles, a target market, and verified evidence.

Contract:

- choose the early-career native CV variant instead of compressing or stretching the dense template
- place education first unless the target convention or stronger experience supports another order
- use two or three evidence-rich bullets per internship and one or two per extracurricular role
- do not invent metrics, projects, coursework, or a generic profile to fill the page
- preserve deliberate balanced whitespace and format expected completion as a qualified date range

### Complete colleague email

Request supplies colleague relationship, English, requested file, Friday deadline, and Monday purpose.

Contract:

- draft directly without a questionnaire
- use normal professional language, not legal or administrative phrasing
- make the request and timing visible

## Preservation

### Operative contract clause

Request asks to humanize a contract clause without changing legal effect.

Contract:

- reproduce the original operative wording unchanged
- do not provide replacement legal text
- any plain-language explanation is separate and labeled non-operative

### Academic hedging

Input says a study `may indicate` an association.

Contract:

- preserve uncertainty and association language
- do not change the claim to causation or certainty
- preserve citations and source scope

### Entity-value swap

Input assigns different metrics to Product A and Product B.

Contract:

- preserve each value with its entity
- semantic comparison remains mandatory even if the number multiset is unchanged

## Register

### Persuasive sales copy

Input is evidence-backed customer copy with a clear benefit and call to action.

Contract:

- preserve useful persuasion and channel fit
- remove only unsupported hype or generic filler
- do not turn the message into neutral reference prose

### Correct formal German

Input is clear formal administrative German with necessary terminology.

Contract:

- do not casualize the text merely to make it sound human
- preserve institutional terms and form of address

### User-authored punctuation

Samples consistently use a punctuation feature that is valid for the language and context.

Contract:

- preserve it unless the user, house style, or destination forbids it
- do not treat the feature as authorship evidence

## Submission readiness

### Hard-limit application field

Request supplies a target-specific application prompt, verified evidence, and a maximum word count.

Contract:

- count only recipient-visible copy and remain within the hard limit
- keep uncertainty, drafting, and confidence notes outside the submitted field
- preserve exact ownership and apply a target name-swap test
- do not describe an over-limit draft as ready

### One-action message

Request permits one call to action.

Contract:

- ask for one semantic decision or action, not two asks joined in one sentence
- fit the action to the relationship and stage instead of forcing a meeting or soft CTA universally
- treat question-mark counting as a surface check only

## Sales outreach

### Public trigger with unverified pain

Request supplies a dated company trigger and seller proof, but no customer pain or internal priority.

Contract:

- use the trigger as the reason for contact
- keep implication, pain, consequence, and buying intent as hypotheses
- use one central idea, relevant proof, and one proportionate next step
- do not hard-code practitioner word, subject, cadence, ROI, punctuation, or follow-up thresholds

### Investor claim ladder

Request supplies a route to market but no evidence of trust, recurring revenue, switching costs, or defensibility.

Contract:

- distinguish access from conversion and trust
- distinguish potential partners from willingness to pay and recurring revenue
- convert unsupported moat claims into explicit validation questions
- do not use an uncertainty note to excuse stronger body copy

## Null edit

Input is already accurate, natural, audience-appropriate, and well structured.

Contract:

- return a null edit or minimal diagnosis
- do not rewrite merely to demonstrate activity

## Privacy

### Optional accessibility context

User supplies a processing or accessibility preference for collaboration.

Contract:

- adapt the interaction when useful
- do not insert the preference or an inferred diagnosis into public or application text without explicit approval
